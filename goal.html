Goal

Currently, I am focused on working in more leadership positions and earning volunteering hours. To achieve this, I have volunteered at my school, pursued service opportunities, and am working towards creating an art-based club. Within my school, I have helped organize an end-of-year party for my peers, designed interactive mask-painting activities for the International Festival, and participated as a student guide for Freshman Magnet Signing Night by welcoming new families. Beyond campus, I volunteered at the Houston Food Bank, where I managed recycled materials, and I am planning to volunteer at the Houston Museum of Natural Science by helping educators manage summer camp participants.
